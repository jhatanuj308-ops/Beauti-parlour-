FAQ

How do I book an appointment?

Tap “Book Appointment”, choose your services, pick a date and time, enter your details and confirm. You will get a booking ID and a WhatsApp confirmation.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes. Open “My Booking”, enter your booking ID and cancel. Please let us know at least 3 hours before your slot.

Do you offer home service?

Yes, home service is available for selected services (extra travel charge applies). Choose “Home Service” while booking.

Do I need to pay in advance?

No advance is needed for regular services. Bridal bookings may need a small token amount to block the date.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Cash, UPI and cards at the parlour.

Do you do bridal trials?

Yes, a trial session is included in our bridal packages.
